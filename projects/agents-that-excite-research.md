## Executive Summary

The AI agent conference space is crowded but fragmented. Most events follow a traditional panels-and-keynotes format. There is a clear gap for a **demo-first, show-don't-tell event** — one where agents perform live on stage rather than get talked about on slides. "Agents That Excite" can own that gap, especially in Chicago where no major agent-focused event exists.

## Gap Analysis: Why Demo-First Wins

### What existing events do:
- **Panels and keynotes** — executives talk about what agents could do
- **Workshop tracks** — hands-on but slow-paced, tutorial-style
- **Expo floors** — booths with sales reps, not live product demos
- **Fireside chats** — interesting but passive consumption

### What nobody is doing:
- **Live agent demos on stage** — agents performing real tasks in real time
- **Head-to-head comparisons** — side-by-side agent showdowns
- **Audience-driven challenges** — crowd picks tasks, agents execute live
- **"Product Hunt meets TED"** format — short, punchy, demo-first presentations
- **Failure-inclusive** — showing what breaks is as valuable as what works

### Why this matters:
1. **Agents are visual** — watching one work is 10x more compelling than hearing about it
2. **Trust gap** — everyone claims their agent is amazing; live demos prove it
3. **Entertainment value** — demos create energy that panels can't match
4. **Shareable content** — live demo clips go viral; panel screenshots don't
5. **Decision-maker friendly** — busy execs want to see, not sit through 8 hours of talks

### Geographic advantage:
- **Chicago has zero major AI agent events** — NYC, SF, LA, and Seattle are saturated
- Central location, strong tech community, lower venue costs than coastal cities
- O'Hare makes it accessible from anywhere in the US

## Recommended Format

### Structure: "Demo Day Meets TED"

**Duration:** 1 day (with optional Day 2 workshop track)

**Main Stage (Day 1):**
- **Morning: "The Showcase"** — 10-minute demo slots. Each speaker gets a live demo + 5 min Q&A. No slides allowed on main stage.
- **Midday: "The Arena"** — Head-to-head agent challenges. Audience picks tasks via live polling. Two agents compete side by side.
- **Afternoon: "The Frontier"** — Research demos and unreleased tech. "Things that will blow your mind in 6 months."
- **Closing: "The Panel"** — One curated 45-min discussion on where agents are headed. Earned by watching demos all day.

**Optional Day 2: Workshops**
- Hands-on agent building sessions
- Enterprise deployment deep-dives
- Higher-priced, smaller groups (50-100 per workshop)
