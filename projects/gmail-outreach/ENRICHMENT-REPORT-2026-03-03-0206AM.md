## 🚨 Key Challenge: Email Verification from Official Sources

**The Core Problem:**
Per instructions: "ONLY use emails found on official published sources. NEVER GUESS email patterns. NEVER hallucinate."

**What We Found:**
1. **Third-party aggregators (RocketReach, ZoomInfo, Lead411, LeadIQ)** provide email patterns
2. **Official PE websites** almost never publish individual email addresses
3. **Team pages** often JavaScript-rendered and don't include contact info
4. **Press releases** rarely include direct email addresses

**What Qualifies as "Official Published Source"?**
- Company website contact pages ✅
- Press releases with quoted contact info ✅
- SEC filings with executive emails ✅
- Conference bios with speaker emails ✅
- Portfolio company announcement PDFs with PE contact ✅
- Third-party aggregators (RocketReach, ZoomInfo) ❌

**Result:** 10 contacts identified with titles and LinkedIn profiles, but 0 emails that qualify under strict verification criteria.
