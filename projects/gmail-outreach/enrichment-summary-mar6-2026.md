## Research Methods & Sources

### Best Sources for Verified Emails:
1. **Emerging Manager Monthly (EMM)** - Direct emails for minority/diverse managers ⭐
2. **ContactOut** - Highest accuracy for direct emails ⭐
3. **RocketReach** - Good for pattern confirmation
4. **Apollo.io** - Mid-level contacts
5. **Official team/bio pages** - Email pattern verification
6. **Conference speaker bios** - Public contact info
7. **SEC filings & press releases** - Executive contacts

### Email Pattern Analysis:
- Standard: `firstinitial+lastname@firm.com`
- Founders often use: `firstname@firm.com`
- Short firm names: `firstinitial+lastname@shortname.vc`
- Examples:
  - `djackson@authorcapital.com`
  - `christopher@burchcreativecapital.com`
  - `ade@base10.vc`
  - `gjain@afore.vc`

### What We Avoided:
❌ Generic inquiry emails (info@, sales@, ir@)  
❌ Unverified pattern guesses  
❌ Contact scraper sites without sources  
❌ Outdated LinkedIn profiles
