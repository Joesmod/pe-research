## Recommendations

### Option 1: LinkedIn InMail Outreach (RECOMMENDED)
**Pros:**
- All 15 contacts have verified LinkedIn profiles
- Professional context
- Higher response rate than cold email to generic addresses
- Can verify identity before outreach

**Cons:**
- Requires LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Premium
- Slower than direct email
- Limited daily InMail quota

### Option 2: Use General Firm Emails
**Approach:**
- Email info@/contact@ addresses
- Address to "Business Development Team" or "Portfolio Operations"
- Request connection with decision-maker

**Pros:**
- Official published addresses
- Compliant with requirements
- Gatekeepers may route to right person

**Cons:**
- Lower response rate
- May get stuck with junior staff
- Longer time to decision-maker

### Option 3: Focus on Smaller/Newer Firms
**Strategy:**
- Target firms <$500M AUM
- Newer firms (founded 2015+)
- Firms with more accessible founding teams

**Rationale:**
- Smaller firms more likely to publish direct contacts
- Founders more accessible
- Less bureaucratic gatekeeping

### Option 4: Paid Data Services
**Services to consider:**
- ZoomInfo (verified business contacts)
- RocketReach Pro (higher accuracy, verified sources)
- ContactOut Premium
- Apollo.io with email credits

**Cost:**
- $100-500/month depending on volume
- May still face verification challenges for top-tier PE firms
