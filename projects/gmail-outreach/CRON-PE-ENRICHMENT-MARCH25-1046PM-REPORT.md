## 🔍 Research Pattern Observed

**Key Finding:** Mid-market PE firms (especially healthcare, services-focused) **do NOT publish individual email addresses** on their websites.

### What IS Publicly Available:
- ✅ LinkedIn profiles
- ✅ Company email domains (from careers/compliance pages)
- ✅ Generic contact emails (IR, BD, info, compliance)
- ✅ Phone numbers (sometimes)
- ✅ Office addresses

### What is NOT Publicly Available:
- ❌ Individual direct email addresses
- ❌ Personal contact information

### Where Individual Emails ARE Found:
- 🔒 Paid contact databases (RocketReach, Wiza, ZoomInfo, Apollo Pro)
- 🔒 Behind paywalls/registration
- 🔒 Partially masked in search results
