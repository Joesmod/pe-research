## How to Use This List

1. **75%+ confidence** = Safe to use for outreach
2. **50-74% confidence** = Use with caution, consider LinkedIn InMail as backup
3. **<50% confidence** = Do not use, find alternative contact method
4. **Official team page** = Highest confidence, use immediately

## Pattern Verification Process

Before adding a pattern to this list:
1. Check LeadIQ, ContactOut, or RocketReach for confidence score
2. Look for official firm website confirmation
3. Cross-reference with LinkedIn profiles
4. Verify at least 2 contacts at the firm use the same pattern
5. Document the source and date

## Notes

- **Never guess** email patterns without verification
- **Always prefer** official sources (firm websites, press releases) over pattern inference
- **Update this file** whenever new patterns are verified
- **Review confidence scores** - they change over time as more data becomes available
