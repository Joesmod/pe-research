## Industry Pattern Analysis

**Why PE firms don't publish emails:**

1. **Deal Flow Control**  
   - Want to filter unsolicited pitches through generic inboxes
   - Assistants screen incoming requests
   - Prevents spam/noise ratio issues

2. **Privacy & Security**  
   - High-net-worth individuals (partners often personally wealthy)
   - Reduce phishing/social engineering attack surface
   - Sensitive deal negotiations require controlled communication

3. **Professional Gatekeeping**  
   - Warm introductions preferred over cold outreach
   - Maintain exclusivity and network-based deal sourcing
   - Only serious/qualified opportunities reach decision-makers

4. **Regulatory Compliance**  
   - SEC regulations around solicitation for private funds
   - Must verify accredited investor status before engagement
   - Generic emails allow compliance team to screen first
