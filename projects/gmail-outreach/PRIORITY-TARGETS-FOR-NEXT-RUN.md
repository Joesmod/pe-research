## Research Strategy for Next Run

### Step 1: Website Review (Quick Filter - 2 min per firm)
- Does site have "Team" or "People" page?
- Any "Business Development" or "Investor Relations" roles listed?
- Is there a "Contact" page with names (not just generic email)?
- Recent press releases or portfolio news?

### Step 2: LinkedIn Search (3 min per firm)
- Search "site:linkedin.com/in [Firm Name] business development"
- Search "site:linkedin.com/in [Firm Name] investor relations"
- Search "site:linkedin.com/in [Firm Name] partner"
- Check company LinkedIn page for employee list

### Step 3: Alternative Sources (If promising - 5 min per firm)
- Google: "[Firm Name] team contact email"
- Check press releases for quoted executives
- Look for conference speaker bios (ACG events, SuperReturn, etc.)
- Search SEC filings if publicly listed portfolio companies

### Step 4: Document & Move On
- If no public email found after 10 min total → mark "Needs LinkedIn InMail"
- If contact found → verify email pattern, note source
- If not PE / wrong fit → mark "Skip"
