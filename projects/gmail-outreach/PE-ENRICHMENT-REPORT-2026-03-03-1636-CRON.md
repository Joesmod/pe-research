## Key Finding
**Large PE firms ($1B+ AUM) rarely publish direct executive emails.** Standard practice:
- Contact forms only
- IR/info@ generic emails
- Press offices for media inquiries
- Direct outreach requires:
  - LinkedIn Sales Navigator
  - Professional databases (ZoomInfo, Apollo - paid tiers)
  - Warm introductions
  - Conference networking
