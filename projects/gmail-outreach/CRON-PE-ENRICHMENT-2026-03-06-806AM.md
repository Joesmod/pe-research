## 📌 Key Findings

### Industry Reality: PE/VC Privacy Practices
Most private equity and venture capital firms **intentionally do not publish direct personal emails** for their partners and managing directors. Instead, they use:

- Generic contact emails (info@, contact@, ir@)
- Contact forms on websites
- LinkedIn for professional outreach
- PR firms for media inquiries

### Why Direct Emails Are Rare:
1. **Spam/solicitation prevention**
2. **Deal flow management** - They want to control inbound channels
3. **Professional gatekeeping** - Assistants and associates filter contacts
4. **Privacy and security concerns**

### Exception Found:
Oak Street Funding (Rick Dennen) published his direct email in a press release because:
- They were hiring a CMO (recruiting/PR context)
- It's a lending/financing company (not pure PE/VC)
- Media relations required direct contact
- 2019 press release for public announcement
