## Key Findings

### Challenge: Email Privacy
- **98% of PE firms** do not publish direct decision-maker emails on their websites
- Third-party data services (RocketReach, ContactOut, ZoomInfo) show masked emails (e.g., c******@firm.com)
- Only generic emails like info@, ir@, or privacy@ are typically public

### Recommended Next Steps
1. **Apollo.io Integration:** Use the Apollo API to find verified direct emails for the contacts identified
2. **LinkedIn Outreach:** Leverage LinkedIn InMails to the contacts identified
3. **Website Contact Forms:** Use official contact forms on PE firm websites
4. **Network Introductions:** Seek warm introductions through mutual connections
5. **Email Pattern Validation:** Use tools like Hunter.io or Clearbit to verify email patterns

### Alternative Approach
Rather than searching for published emails (which rarely exist), the enrichment process should:
1. Identify the RIGHT decision-makers (✅ Completed for 9 firms)
2. Find their LinkedIn profiles (✅ Completed for 9 firms)
3. Use Apollo.io or similar tools to find verified emails (⏳ Next step)
4. Validate emails before sending (⏳ Next step)
