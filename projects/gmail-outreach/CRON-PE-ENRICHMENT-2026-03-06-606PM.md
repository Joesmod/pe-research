## 🎯 ENRICHMENT METHODOLOGY

### Successful Patterns:
1. **LinkedIn verification** - Most reliable for current roles  
2. **Official team pages** - Best for email patterns and titles  
3. **RocketReach/ZoomInfo** - Confirms email format patterns  
4. **Bloomberg/Crunchbase** - Good for title verification  
5. **Cross-verification** - Always check 2+ sources before confirming  

### Email Pattern Observations:
- **Most common:** [first_initial][last]@domain.com (e.g., rdelaney@)  
- **Simple format:** [first]@domain.com (e.g., harry@, brian@, eric@)  
- **Abbreviated:** [first_initial][last_initial]@domain.com (e.g., rs@)  
- **Full name:** [first].[last]@domain.com (rare for PE firms)  

### Red Flags Encountered:
- ❌ Zero search results for name + firm = likely outdated contact  
- ❌ LinkedIn shows contact at different company = wrong info  
- ⚠️ Only 1 weak source = needs additional verification
