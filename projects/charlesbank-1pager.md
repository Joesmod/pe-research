## How We Work

Gumbo embeds AI directly into portfolio company operations — not as consultants writing decks, but as builders shipping working systems. Our model:

1. **Identify high-leverage workflows** — We audit portfolio company operations for automation opportunities with the highest ROI: sales outreach, data processing, customer ops, internal tooling.

2. **Build custom AI agents** — Not off-the-shelf SaaS. Purpose-built agents that integrate with existing systems (CRMs, ERPs, communication tools) and execute real workflows autonomously.

3. **Deploy and iterate** — Agents go live in days, not quarters. We monitor, tune, and expand scope as they prove value.
