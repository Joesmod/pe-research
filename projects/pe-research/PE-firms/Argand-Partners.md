## Overview
Argand Partners is a private equity firm that acquires controlling interests in middle-market industrial businesses with established market leadership positions and track records of stable cash flows. Founded by former senior investment team members of Castle Harlan Inc.

## AUM
Mid-market focus

## Investment Focus
- **Sectors:** Industrials, industrial-related energy, consumer, manufacturing and services
- **Geography:** Primarily North America
- **Deal Type:** Middle-market buyouts, controlling interests
- **Target Profile:** Established market leaders with stable cash flows
