# Arctos Partners

**Website:** https://www.arctospartners.com  
**LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/arctospartners  
**Location:** Dallas, TX and New York, NY  
**Type:** Private investment firm (sports franchises & PE funds)

## Overview
Arctos is a private investment firm focused on creating valuable solutions to complex problems, particularly in professional sports franchises and alternative asset management. The firm has pioneered private capital solutions for sports team ownership and PE fund secondaries.

## AUM
$3B+ (Fund I announced with more than $3.0 billion in 2021)

## Investment Focus
- **Sports Strategy:** Minority stakes in professional sports franchises (North American sports, European soccer)
- **Keystone Strategy:** Alternative asset managers, their funds and portfolio companies
- Innovation in traditionally complex and illiquid markets
