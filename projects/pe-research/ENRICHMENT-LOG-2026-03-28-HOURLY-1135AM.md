## Key Learnings

1. **Official team pages are gold:** Many firms list emails directly on their /team/ or /people/ pages
2. **PDF tearsheets/fact sheets:** Often contain direct contact info
3. **Email pattern verification:** When 3+ sources confirm a pattern (e.g., firstlast@domain), it's reliable
4. **Bain Capital pattern:** [first_initial][last]@baincapital.com (88% adoption per RocketReach)
5. **LinkedIn alone is insufficient:** Must find verified email from published source
6. **Generic emails (info@, ir@) are low-value:** Priority should be direct decision-maker emails
