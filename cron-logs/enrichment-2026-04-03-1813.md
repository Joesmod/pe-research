## Research Methodology

**Sources Searched:**
- Official company websites (team pages, contact pages, press releases)
- LinkedIn profiles
- PR Newswire, Business Wire press releases
- SEC filings (where applicable)
- Industry publications

**Avoided Sources:**
- Paid data providers (RocketReach, Apollo, ZoomInfo, etc.) - emails shown are obfuscated
- Email pattern guessing
- Unverified contact databases

## Key Findings

1. **Most PE firms do NOT publish direct personal emails publicly** for security/privacy
2. **Verified emails found only when:**
   - Individual bio pages include email (WILsquare)
   - Press releases list direct contact for deals (Argosy)
   - Contact pages show general firm emails (Nonantum)

3. **Common obstacles:**
   - Contact forms instead of emails
   - Only generic info@/contact@ addresses
   - Phone numbers without email addresses
   - Data provider obfuscation (partial emails: e******@platteriverequity.com)

## Recommendations

**For firms without direct emails:**
- LinkedIn outreach to verified contacts
- Phone calls to main office requesting appropriate contact
- Use generic email with personalized message referencing specific person/title
- Monitor for press releases that may include contact info
- Network through mutual connections
