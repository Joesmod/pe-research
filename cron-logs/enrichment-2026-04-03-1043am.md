## 📊 Key Insights

### Challenge: Limited Published Emails
**Finding:** Most mid-market PE firms do NOT publish individual decision-maker emails on their official websites.
- Team pages list names & titles but no direct emails
- Contact pages provide generic firm emails (info@, ir@, contact@)
- Third-party databases (RocketReach, ContactOut) have email patterns but these are NOT official sources per instructions

**Only Exception:** Trivest Partners published `info@trivest.com` in their official PDF factsheet.

**Implication:** For future outreach, may need to:
1. Use firm general emails (info@, contact@)
2. Infer standard patterns ONLY after verifying at least 2 examples from official sources (press releases, SEC filings)
3. Use LinkedIn InMail or company contact forms
