## Recommendation: Update Enrichment Protocol

### Proposed 3-Tier System

**Tier 1 - Official Published** (Rare)
- Emails on firm website/SEC filings/press releases
- Mark: "Verified from [official source]"

**Tier 2 - Pattern Inferred** (Common, Safe)
- When 1+ verified email exists at firm, infer pattern for other verified employees
- Example: If ceo@acme.com confirmed → partner John Smith = jsmith@acme.com
- Mark: "Inferred from verified pattern"
- **This is what most of the sheet already uses**

**Tier 3 - Database Source** (Use with caution)
- ContactOut/RocketReach/Apollo emails
- Mark: "From [source] - verify before outreach"
- Only when role/title confirmed on LinkedIn

### Why This Matters
**Current policy makes enrichment impossible.** No PE firm publishes partner emails publicly. This is intentional - they want warm intros, not cold outreach.
