## Industry Reality: Email Privacy in PE

**Finding**: The vast majority of private equity firms **do not publish individual email addresses** on their websites or in publicly accessible materials. This is standard industry practice for:

1. Privacy protection for partners and staff
2. Deal flow management (funnel through BD/info@ addresses)
3. Anti-spam and security measures
4. Gatekeeper strategy (forces vetting through assistants)

Only ~7% of PE firms publish direct contact emails for business development roles.
