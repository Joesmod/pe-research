## Research Methodology

### Search Strategy
1. **Official Website Review**: Checked firm websites for team/about pages with contact information
2. **LinkedIn Verification**: Cross-referenced names and titles on company LinkedIn pages
3. **Email Pattern Analysis**: Identified email domains where available (e.g., @ppcpartners.com, @invcg.com, @oceanavenuecapital.com)
4. **Third-Party Cross-Check**: Used Crunchbase, PitchBook, and industry directories to verify roles when official sites lacked detail
5. **SEC Form ADV**: Attempted to locate regulatory filings for contact verification (where applicable)

### Email Verification Criteria
- **✅ Verified**: Email found on official company website or direct official source
- **⚠️ Pattern Only**: Email domain confirmed, but individual email not publicly listed
- **❌ Not Found**: No direct email available; only generic addresses (info@, contact@) or no email at all

### Exclusions
- **No guessing of email patterns**: Even where patterns were identified (@ppcpartners.com, @mbclp.com), specific emails were NOT inferred without direct official confirmation
- **No hallucinated emails**: Left blank if not found on published official sources
