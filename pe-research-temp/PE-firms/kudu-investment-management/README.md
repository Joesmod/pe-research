## Firm Profile
Kudu provides long-term capital solutions to independent asset and wealth managers globally, including:
- Generational ownership transfers
- Management buyouts
- Acquisition and growth finance
- Liquidity for legacy partners

Unlike other capital solutions, Kudu does not seek exits on its investments and partners with firms for the long term.
