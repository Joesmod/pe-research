## Firm Overview

Cressey & Company is a private equity firm focused exclusively on healthcare investments. The firm was formed in 2008 following the separation of Thoma Cressey Bravo into three independent firms. Bryan Cressey has 40+ years of healthcare investing experience dating back to founding teams at Golder, Thoma, Cressey, Rauner.
