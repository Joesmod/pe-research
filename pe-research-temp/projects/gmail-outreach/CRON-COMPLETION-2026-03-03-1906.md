## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Sheet Quality Issue
**~33% of "unresearched" firms are not PE firms** - they're service providers (recruiters, consultants, advisors) who WORK WITH PE firms. Recommend a data cleanup pass.

### 2. Large PE Firms Don't Publish Emails
Established mega-funds (Apax, CD&R, etc.) rarely publish individual partner emails. They use:
- General inquiry forms
- PR firm contacts
- Office phone numbers
- LinkedIn as primary outreach channel

### 3. Better Sources Needed
For difficult enrichments, consider:
- **Apollo.io**: Verified contact database (paid)
- **SEC filings**: Form D filings sometimes include emails
- **Press releases**: PR contacts for media inquiries
- **Conference speaker lists**: Sometimes include emails
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator**: Direct InMail option

### 4. Email Patterns Are Unreliable
Third-party tools (RocketReach, ZoomInfo) guess patterns but shouldn't be trusted without verification. Lead Edge Capital was the only firm where we confirmed the pattern from an official source.
