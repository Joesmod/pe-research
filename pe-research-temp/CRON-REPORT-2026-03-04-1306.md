## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS

### What Worked Well:
1. **Strict verification standards** - Only using official sources ensured high quality
2. **Senior-level focus** - All contacts are C-level or Partner level
3. **Detailed documentation** - Created comprehensive dossiers for verified contacts
4. **Systematic approach** - Firm websites → team pages → individual bios → contact info

### Challenges Encountered:
1. **Many firms hide emails** - Even senior executives often not published on official sites
2. **Third-party data unreliable** - ZoomInfo, RocketReach show partial emails but not verifiable
3. **Dynamic content issues** - Some team pages use JavaScript, harder to scrape
4. **Time vs. Volume tradeoff** - Deep research takes time; chose quality over quantity

### Recommendations for Next Session:
1. **Focus on press releases** - Often contain email addresses for media inquiries
2. **SEC filings** - May have executive contact info for public or recently-public firms
3. **Conference speaker bios** - Industry events often publish speaker emails
4. **Portfolio company announcements** - Investment announcements sometimes include PE firm contact
5. **LinkedIn outreach** - For partial enrichments, LinkedIn InMail may be necessary
