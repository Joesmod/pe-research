## Research Methodology

### Sources Used:
1. **Web Search:** Brave Search API for firm discovery and verification
2. **Official Websites:** Team pages, about sections, contact pages
3. **LinkedIn:** Profile verification and role confirmation
4. **Apollo.io:** Attempted contact searches (limited success - most firms don't publish direct emails)
5. **Industry Publications:** Private Equity International, Crunchbase, business news

### Contact Discovery Strategy:
- Searched firm websites for team pages
- Verified titles and roles via LinkedIn
- Cross-referenced press releases and news announcements
- Prioritized Partners, Managing Directors, Co-Heads of relevant sectors (Healthcare, Technology)
- **Email Policy:** Only included emails from official published sources (no guessing patterns)

### What We Did NOT Do:
- ❌ Guess email patterns (e.g., firstname.lastname@firm.com)
- ❌ Use unverified third-party contact databases
- ❌ Hallucinate contact information
- ❌ Include outdated or uncertain information
