## Key Learnings

### Pattern Recognition - How to Spot Non-PE Firms Faster:

**🚩 Red Flags (Not PE Investors):**
- Keywords: "platform," "software," "SaaS," "technology," "solutions," "marketplace"
- They describe "customers" or "clients" rather than portfolio companies
- Website has pricing pages, product demos, or "request a demo" buttons
- Team backgrounds in tech/software rather than investing/operations
- Revenue model is subscriptions/fees rather than carried interest

**✅ Green Lights (Legitimate PE):**
- Portfolio companies listed
- Investment criteria clearly stated (EBITDA range, sectors)
- Managing Partners/Investment Committee listed
- Fund history and AUM disclosed
- Focus on value creation, operational improvement, partnerships

### Email Research Strategy:

1. **First Check:** RocketReach, ContactOut, ZoomInfo (most reliable)
2. **Second Check:** Company team pages + LinkedIn
3. **Pattern Inference:** Only use if multiple sources confirm pattern
4. **When Stuck:** Use general contact (info@) with note that direct email not publicly available
