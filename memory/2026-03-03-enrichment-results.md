## Key Finding
**PE firms intentionally shield executive contacts** to avoid spam. Published emails are extremely rare except for:
- Investor relations generics (ir@, info@)
- Some junior staff (VPs, Associates)
- Firms with strong operational/portfolio support teams

## Recommendations

### For Future Enrichment:
1. **Focus on operational roles**: VPs, Directors of BD/Portfolio Ops more likely to have published emails
2. **Check portfolio company boards**: PE partners often listed on portfolio co websites with emails
3. **Conference speaker bios**: Industry events sometimes publish contact info
4. **Press releases**: Announce new hires/promotions with emails
5. **SEC filings**: For public portfolio companies, PE board members listed
6. **LinkedIn InMail**: Alternative to email for initial outreach

### Better Target Profile:
- **Mid-market PE with operational teams** (not just deal teams)
- **Firms with active portfolio support** (value creation, digital transformation)
- **Service-heavy industries** (healthcare IT, business services, SaaS)
- **Growth equity** (more accessible than buyout)

### Next Steps:
1. Update CRM with researched firm intelligence (even without emails)
2. Create GitHub dossiers for all researched firms
3. Target 3-5 NEW firms with better operational visibility
4. Consider LinkedIn outreach strategy as email alternative
