## Research Methodology

1. **Primary Sources Prioritized**:
   - Company official websites (contact pages, team pages, news releases)
   - SEC filings (for financial institutions)
   - Press releases on company websites
   - Industry databases (Private Equity International)

2. **Email Validation Approach**:
   - Only used emails found on official public sources
   - Never guessed email patterns without verification
   - Inferred patterns only when multiple examples confirmed the format
   - Left blank when no verified email could be found

3. **Decision-Maker Criteria**:
   - C-level executives (CEO, CTO, COO, CMO, CFO)
   - Managing Partners, General Partners, Operating Partners
   - Directors (Technology, Operations, Value Creation, BD)
   - VPs (Technology, Digital Transformation, Portfolio Ops)
   - Heads of Value Creation, Business Development
   - Media/PR contacts when specific decision-makers not publicly available
