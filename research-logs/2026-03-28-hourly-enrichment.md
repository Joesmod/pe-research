### Key Finding

1. Official PDF tearsheets/factsheets
2. Press release contact sections

## Recommendations

1. **For future enrichment:** Focus on smaller/newer PE firms (<$1B AUM) that are more likely to publish contact info

3. **PDF strategy:** Search for PE firms that publish annual reports, factsheets, or tearsheets - these occasionally include contact info
4. **Press release mining:** Search BusinessWire, PRNewswire for recent PE announcements with contact sections
5. **Conference materials:** Check PE conference speaker lists, panel bios, downloadable PDFs
