## 3. Gap Analysis / Differentiation Angle

### The Gap
The current AI event landscape has a fundamental problem: **audiences don't believe the hype**. After two years of "AI will transform everything" keynotes, buyers are skeptical. They've seen the slides. They want receipts.

### The Differentiation: "Show Don't Tell" at Scale
**"Agents that Excite"** fills this gap by making the demo the main event:

- **Every speaker slot is a live demo** — no slides-only presentations allowed
- **Real agents doing real work** — not toy demos, but production workflows (outreach, research, CRM automation, code generation, customer service)
- **Chicago-native** — positioned for the Midwest enterprise buyer who flies to SF/NYC reluctantly and would love a local flagship
- **PE/VC-friendly format** — operating partners and portfolio CTOs see what's deployable *today*, not what's coming in 18 months

### Why No One's Doing This
- Live demos are risky — agents can fail on stage
- Most event organizers are content companies, not builders
- Requires a network of teams actually running production agents (Gumbo has this)

### Our Unfair Advantage
Gumbo's agent swarm *is* the demo. We're not curating third-party content — we're showcasing what we build, alongside handpicked teams who actually ship.

## 6. Recommendation

### Format
- **Half-day event (4-5 hours)** — afternoon into evening, ending with networking + drinks
- **8-10 live demo slots** (15 min each, strict time, live or nothing)
- **"Agent Arena" expo area** — small booths where teams show agents in real-time, attendees interact
