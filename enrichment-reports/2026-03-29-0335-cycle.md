## Key Findings

### Email Verification Challenge
**Problem:** Most mid-market PE firms do NOT publish direct email addresses on their official websites.

**What we found:**
- Generic contact emails only: info@, ir@, general inquiries
- Team/leadership pages list names and titles but NO emails
- Contact pages have forms or generic addresses
- Email pattern databases (ContactOut, ZoomInfo, RocketReach) *suggest* patterns but these are:
  - Inferred from algorithms, not verified published sources
  - Against our enrichment policy (NEVER GUESS)

**Next steps:**
- Use Apollo.io API for verified contact lookups
- Search for press releases with "for more information contact"
- Check SEC filings, conference bios, PDFs
- LinkedIn DMs for connection requests (but no email scraping)
