## Key Findings & Challenges

### Industry Pattern: Email Privacy
**PE firms rarely publish individual email addresses publicly.** Standard practice includes:
- Generic emails (info@, contact@, ir@) for initial outreach
- Individual emails only accessible through paid databases (Apollo, ZoomInfo, RocketReach)
- Third-party aggregators show obfuscated emails requiring enrichment credits

### Apollo API Limitations
- API shows contacts with `has_email: true` but actual emails are obfuscated
- Requires additional "enrichment" API calls (costs credits)
- Free/basic tier insufficient for PE contact research

### Recommended Approach for Future Enrichment

#### Option 1: Use Paid Databases
- Apollo.io enrichment API (requires credits)
- ZoomInfo (subscription required)
- RocketReach (per-contact pricing)

#### Option 2: Direct Outreach via Generic Emails
- Many PE firms route info@ emails to appropriate teams
- Generic emails are officially published and acceptable for cold outreach
- Follow up with phone calls to reach decision-makers

#### Option 3: LinkedIn Outreach
- LinkedIn profiles publicly available for most PE professionals
- InMail or connection requests for direct contact
- Often more effective than email for PE professionals
