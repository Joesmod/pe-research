## Research Methods (Priority Order)

### 1. Company Website - Team/Leadership Pages
- Look for `/team`, `/leadership`, `/about`, `/contact` pages
- Many PE firms list partners with contact info
- Check for individual partner pages with bios

### 2. LinkedIn Direct Search
- Search: `"[Person Name]" "[Company Name]"`
- Check their profile for contact info in summary or experience
- Look for email patterns in connections/posts

### 3. Press Releases & Conference Bios
- Google: `"[Person Name]" "[Company]" email`
- Check PR Newswire, Business Wire for contact info
- Conference speaker bios often include emails

### 4. SEC Filings & Investor Relations
- For public firms or those with public portfolio companies
- Form D filings, 13F filings list key personnel
- IR pages often have direct contacts

### 5. Company PDFs & Downloadable Materials
- Google: `site:[website] filetype:pdf contact`
- Annual reports, brochures, pitch decks may have emails
- Look for "For more information contact:"

### 6. Email Pattern Verification
- Once you find one confirmed email, test the pattern
- Common patterns: first.last@, firstl@, flast@
- Never guess - only use confirmed patterns
