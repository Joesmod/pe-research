## Key Findings

1. **Email Publishing Trend:** Mid-market PE firms (especially those sub-$5B AUM) almost never publish individual email addresses

2. **Common Patterns:**
   - Partner/MD names readily available on "Team" pages
   - Titles clearly listed
   - LinkedIn profiles findable
   - **But emails deliberately withheld**

3. **Alternative Contact Methods:**
   - Website contact forms (universally available)
   - Main office phone numbers
   - Generic emails (info@, contact@, bd@)
   - LinkedIn direct messages

4. **Firms That DO Publish Emails:**
   - Very rare
   - Usually only for:
     * Press/media contacts
     * Investor relations
     * Legal/compliance
   - Almost never for deal origination/BD

## Recommendations

### For Immediate Outreach:
1. **Use firm generic emails** where available (info@, contact@)
2. **Call main office** and ask for BD/deal origination team
3. **LinkedIn outreach** to verified contacts (most effective for PE)
4. **Industry conferences** - PE professionals attend regularly

### For Sheet Updates:
Given the research constraints, recommend updating Status field to:
- **"Researched - Contact via LinkedIn"** for firms with verified leadership but no published emails
- **"Researched - Use Main Office"** for firms with phone/generic email
- Include **Notes** with verified leadership names and titles

### Alternative Approach:
**Focus enrichment efforts on:**
1. Firms that actually publish emails (rare, but they exist)
2. Portfolio companies (often more accessible)
3. Recent deal press releases (sometimes include contact emails)
4. Firms with dedicated BD/origination teams that welcome inbound

## Conclusion

The "no email guessing" policy is sound for quality/compliance, but it creates a practical challenge: **most PE firms deliberately don't publish individual emails.**

**Recommendation:** Define "official published source" to include:
- Press release contact lines
- SEC filings (for public companies)
- Industry directory listings (ACG, PitchBook, etc.)
- LinkedIn **if email is listed on profile**
- Business card scans from events

This would expand the pool while maintaining quality standards.
