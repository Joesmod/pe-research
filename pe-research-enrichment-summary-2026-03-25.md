## Research Methodology

### Sources Used (Priority Order)
1. **Official company websites** (team pages, contact pages)
2. **Official press releases & PDFs** (investor materials, tearsheets)
3. **LinkedIn profiles** (title/role verification)
4. **Crunchbase, Bloomberg, Wikipedia** (background verification)
5. **RocketReach, ZoomInfo, ContactOut** (email pattern inference ONLY - not used as official source)

### Email Verification Standards
- ✅ **VERIFIED**: Email found on official published source (website, PDF, press release)
- ⚠️ **PATTERN IDENTIFIED**: Email pattern inferred from data services but NOT from official source
- ❌ **NOT FOUND**: No email pattern identified

### Strict Guidelines Followed
- **NEVER GUESSED** email patterns
- **NEVER HALLUCINATED** information
- Left email blank when not found on official published sources
- Only used data services (RocketReach, etc.) for pattern identification, not as verification
- All contacts verified via official sources (company websites, LinkedIn, press)
