## Pattern Recognition

**Common Patterns for Non-PE Firms:**
1. Keywords: "platform," "software," "SaaS," "technology," "solutions," "marketplace"
2. They describe themselves as serving PE/VC firms rather than investing in companies
3. Revenue model is subscriptions/fees rather than carried interest/management fees
4. Team backgrounds in tech/software rather than investing/operations
5. Website focuses on product features rather than portfolio companies

**How to Spot Them Faster:**
- Check "About Us" page: If they talk about "customers" or "clients" (PE firms), they're likely a service provider
- Look for pricing pages, product demos, or "request a demo" - PE firms don't have these
- Check if they list portfolio companies or client testimonials - testimonials = service provider
