# LFM Capital

**Location:** Nashville, TN  
**Website:** https://www.lfmcapital.com  
**Founded:** 2014  
**Type:** Lower Middle Market Private Equity  

## Overview
LFM Capital is a Nashville-based private equity firm founded by operators and engineers with a focus on investing in manufacturing companies across the U.S. and Canada. The firm emphasizes operational excellence and establishes long-term partnerships with management teams.

## Investment Focus
- **Sector:** Manufacturing, Industrial Services
- **Geography:** U.S. and Canada
- **Deal Size:** Lower middle market
- **Strategy:** Operator-first approach, operational value creation
