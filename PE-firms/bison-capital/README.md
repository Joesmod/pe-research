# Bison Capital Asset Management

## Overview
Bison Capital Asset Management is a private equity firm that provides flexible growth capital solutions to middle-market companies. Founded in 2001, the firm makes growth equity investments in fundamentally strong companies, both public and private, for expansion, acquisitions, or recapitalizations.

## Key Information
- **Founded**: 2001
- **Headquarters**: Santa Monica, CA
- **Additional Office**: Delray Beach, FL
- **Website**: https://bisoncapital.com
- **Stage**: Lower middle market growth capital
- **Investment Type**: Control and non-control investments

## Investment Strategy
- **Capital Solutions**: Flexible growth capital, customized to business needs
- **Deal Types**: Expansion, acquisitions, recapitalizations
- **Company Profile**: Fundamentally strong middle-market companies
- **Structure**: Public or private companies
- **Approach**: Supportive capital with buy-and-build value creation strategy
