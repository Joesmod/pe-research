## Background
William (Bill) Ackman founded Pershing Square Capital Management in 2004. He is one of the most well-known activist investors in the world, known for taking significant positions in companies and pushing for strategic changes.

Pershing Square operates as an investment management firm providing advisory services to multinational corporations on mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, restructurings, and other strategic corporate transactions. The firm also manages and invests capital on behalf of institutional and high net-worth investors.
