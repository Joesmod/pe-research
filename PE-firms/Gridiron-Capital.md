## Investment Strategy

Gridiron Capital is an investment firm focused on partnering with founders, entrepreneurs, and management teams. They create value by building middle-market companies into industry leaders in:
- Branded consumer
- B2B and B2C services
- Niche industrial segments

Operating primarily in the United States and Canada.

## Key Differentiator

As a team led by former operators and entrepreneurs, Gridiron Capital brings hands-on experience in building and running businesses. They emphasize:
- Operational excellence
- "Gridiron Playbook" methodology
- Centers of Excellence program
- Partnerships grounded in shared values
