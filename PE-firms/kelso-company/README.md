## Investment Strategy
Kelso & Company is one of the most experienced firms in private equity, focused on middle-market North American companies. The firm specializes in:
- Financial services
- Business services
- Healthcare
- Consumer products
- Industrial sectors
- Media
- Energy

Investment focus includes both buyout and growth capital transactions.
