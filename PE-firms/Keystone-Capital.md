## Business
Keystone Capital is a lower middle-market private equity firm focused on "buy and build" consolidation strategies. The firm invests personal (not institutional) capital, allowing for a true partnership structure with business owner/operators and management teams. They pursue opportunities where they can execute consolidations, drive organic and inorganic growth, improve operational efficiencies, and build life-long relationships with founders and management teams.
