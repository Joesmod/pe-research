## Overview

Wind Point Partners is a Chicago-based private equity investment firm with approximately $6 billion in assets under management. The firm focuses on partnering with top-caliber management teams to build market-leading companies in the consumer and industrial sectors. With over 40 years of operating history, Wind Point has a proven track record and highly experienced team.

## Investment Strategy

- **Sector:** Consumer products, business services, industrial products
- **Geography:** Primarily North America
- **Deal Size:** Lower-to-middle market companies
- **Approach:** Control buyouts with active partnership model
- **Philosophy:** "Partner of choice" for business owners and executives
