# JLL Partners

**Location:** New York, NY  
**Website:** https://www.jllpartners.com  
**AUM:** $5B+ (as of 2024)

## Overview
JLL Partners is a leading private equity firm focused on investing in growing businesses serving niche markets with defensible competitive positions. Founded in 1988, the firm has completed over 100 investments across its 30+ year history.

## Focus Areas
- Healthcare & Life Sciences
- Business Services
- Consumer
- Technology & Industrial Services

## Investment Criteria
- Enterprise value: $500M - $2B
- Revenue: $100M+
- EBITDA: $25M+
- Market leadership in niche sectors
- Platform and add-on acquisition opportunities

## Investment Approach
- Control buyout transactions
- Partnership with strong management teams
- Operational value creation through JLL Resources Group
- Build platforms through strategic add-ons
- Long-term value creation focus

## Key Strengths
- 30+ years of middle-market investing experience
- Deep domain expertise in core sectors
- Proven operational improvement playbook
- Strong network in business services and healthcare
