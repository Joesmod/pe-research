## Overview

Gemspring Capital is a Westport, Connecticut-based private equity firm providing flexible capital solutions to lower middle market companies. With $5.1 billion of capital under management, the firm partners with talented management teams to drive growth, operational excellence, and strategic value creation. Founded in 2015 by Bret Wiener, former Managing Director at H.I.G. Capital.
