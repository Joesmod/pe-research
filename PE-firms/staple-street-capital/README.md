## Investment Strategy
Staple Street Capital is a middle-market private equity firm specializing in complex transactions where operational expertise and creative structuring add value. The firm focuses on:
- **Corporate Carve-outs**: Divesting non-core divisions from larger corporations
- **Family-owned Business Transitions**: Succession planning and generational transfers
- **Operational Restructurings**: Turnaround situations requiring operational improvement
- **Balance Sheet Restructurings**: Companies needing capital structure optimization
- **Refinancings & Recapitalizations**: Strategic financial restructuring

The firm partners with management teams during inflection points to build stronger, more valuable businesses.
