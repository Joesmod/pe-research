## Investment Strategy
One Equity Partners invests in middle market businesses across three core sectors: industrial, healthcare, and technology. The firm targets companies with enterprise values between $500M-$5B, positioning itself below mega-funds ($10B+ deals) but above typical lower middle market firms (sub-$1B transactions).

**Target Sectors:**
- **Industrial**: Manufacturing, distribution, business services in industrial verticals
- **Healthcare**: Healthcare services, healthcare IT, related healthcare businesses
- **Technology**: Software, tech-enabled services, technology infrastructure

The firm's differentiation comes from its middle-market scale combined with cross-border capabilities (North America + Europe), allowing it to support companies with international growth ambitions.
