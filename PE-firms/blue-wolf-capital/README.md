## Overview
Blue Wolf Capital Partners LLC is a private equity firm founded in 2005 by Adam Blumenthal and Josh Wolf-Powers. The firm has raised four funds to date and manages over $2B in assets. Blue Wolf drives operational excellence through innovative solutions for middle-market companies.

## Investment Approach
Pairs experienced investment professionals with veteran operators to create value through operational excellence, navigating regulatory challenges, and building strong labor partnerships.
