## Investment Strategy
Huron Capital is a lower middle-market private equity firm focused on buy-and-build strategies in North American services businesses. They specialize in:
- Infrastructure Services
- Facility Services  
- Residential Services

They bring a people-first, thematic approach to private equity investing in secularly relevant and fragmented services sectors.

## Key Features
- **ExecFactor® Program**: Proprietary thematic investment strategy where they underwrite an industry and partner with an executive to deploy their buy-and-build investment model
- 290+ platform and add-on businesses partnered with since inception
- Aim to invest $50 million or more of equity in each business
