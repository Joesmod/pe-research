## Overview

Huron Capital Partners is a Detroit-based private equity firm founded in 1999, specializing in services-focused investments with a people-first, thematic, and buy-and-build approach. The firm has managed private equity funds aggregating nearly $2.0 billion in committed capital and has supported over 290 platform and add-on companies across North America.
