# Renovus Capital

## Overview
Renovus Capital is an investment firm focused on the "Knowledge & Talent" industries, spanning education, healthcare services, technology services, and professional services. The firm typically partners with founder-led businesses, leveraging industry expertise and access to capital to drive operational improvements and strategic growth.

## Key Information
- **Founded**: 2010
- **Headquarters**: Wayne, PA (Greater Philadelphia area)
- **Website**: https://renovuscapital.com
- **AUM**: $2+ billion across four funds
- **Portfolio**: 30+ U.S.-based businesses

## Investment Strategy
- **Sector Focus**:

  - Technology Services
  - Professional Services
- **Stage**: Founder-led businesses, middle market
- **Approach**: Operational improvements, talent recruitment, add-on acquisitions, strategic growth
- **Capital Access**: Both debt and equity capital solutions
