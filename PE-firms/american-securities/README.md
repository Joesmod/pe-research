# American Securities

**Location:** New York, NY  
**Website:** https://www.american-securities.com  
**AUM:** $21B+ (as of 2024)

## Overview
American Securities is a leading U.S. private equity firm that invests in market-leading North American companies with annual revenues generally ranging from $200 million to $2 billion. Founded in 1947, the firm has a long history of successful middle-market buyouts.

## Focus Areas
- Industrials
- Healthcare
- Business Services
- Consumer
- Media & Communications

## Investment Strategy
- Control-oriented middle-market buyouts
- Target revenue: $200M - $2B
- Partnership approach with management
- Strong focus on operational value creation
- "Flex Equity" strategy for flexible capital structures

## Key Strengths
- 75+ year operating history
- Deep operational expertise through American Securities Operating Partners (ASOPs)
- Proven track record in services-heavy industries
- Strong relationships with management teams and intermediaries

## Portfolio Highlights
- Typical holding period: 5-7 years
- Active portfolio company support through ASOP executives
- Value creation through organic growth and strategic add-ons
