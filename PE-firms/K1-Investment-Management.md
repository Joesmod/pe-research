## Overview
K1 Investment Management is one of the largest investors in small-cap AI-powered software and enterprise software companies. Founded in 2011 by Hasan Askari at age 26, K1 partners early with founders and management teams to build category leaders that deploy AI-powered, mission-critical systems of record for enterprise clients globally. The firm applies a people, process, and systems methodology to scale investments with the same rigor they bring to portfolio companies.
