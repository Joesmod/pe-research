## Investment Strategy
New Harbor Capital is a Chicago-based private equity firm focused exclusively on lower middle market investments in three core sectors: healthcare, education, and technology-enabled services. The firm emphasizes operational partnerships with management teams and leverages a network of Executive Advisors to drive value creation.

**Key Statistics:**
- 90+ years of combined partner experience
- 230+ investments completed since 2000
- Strong operational value-add through Executive Advisor network

**Target Sectors:**
- **Healthcare**: Providers, outsourced services, pharmaceutical services, wellness businesses
- **Education**: K-12, post-secondary, skills gap training, corporate learning platforms
- **Technology-Enabled Services**: Tech-enabled service businesses across various verticals

The firm's sweet spot is the intersection of these sectors—companies leveraging technology to deliver healthcare or education services at scale.
