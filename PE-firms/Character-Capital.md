# Character Capital

## Overview
- **Type**: Venture Capital (Seed/Pre-Seed Specialist)
- **Founded**: 2017
- **Location**: San Francisco Bay Area / Distributed
- **Website**: https://character.vc
- **Focus**: Pre-seed and seed stage software startups at frontiers of AI and software-addressable economy
- **Tagline**: "Supporting startups with capital and sprints"

## Investment Philosophy
- Early-stage technical founders building at frontiers of software
- AI-enabled expansion of software into previously analog sectors
- Hands-on support via sprint methodologies
- "Clarity about what to do, and validation when it's working"
- Integrity and character-driven partnerships

## Investment Strategy
- **Check Size (Core)**: Up to $2M for 6-10% ownership
- **Check Size (Labs)**: $150k for 5% ownership
- **Stage**: Pre-seed, seed, occasionally inception
- **Solo Founders**: Yes (will invest)
- **Board Seats**: Occasionally for Core Investments, not required
- **Pro Rata**: Guaranteed for Character Labs companies

## Key Programs

### Character Labs
- Startup accelerator 100% focused on product-market fit
- 2x per year cohorts
- Up to 15 teams per cohort
- $150k investment per company
- Back-to-back sprints with real customer testing each week

### Character Foundations
- One-day masterclass for founders
- Product design and marketing approach
- Off-the-record conversations about building in age of AI

## Sprint Methodologies

### Design Sprint (5 days)
- Rapidly prototype and test ideas with real customers
- Created by Jake Knapp at Google in 2010
- Featured in bestselling book "Sprint"

### Foundation Sprint (2 days)
- Starting big projects
- Radical differentiation and opinionated marketing
- Tailor-made for pre-seed/seed stage teams

### Name Sprint
- Company/product naming in ~1 day

## Investment Thesis
- AI has dramatically expanded what's possible with software

- Early customer validation is critical (show, don't ask)
- Product-market fit = consistently and sustainably find, convert, service, and retain customers
