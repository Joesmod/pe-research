## Investment Strategy
Brighton Park Capital is a growth equity firm that specializes in entrepreneur-led, innovative technology companies. The firm focuses on:
- Software / SaaS
- Information services
- Technology-enabled services
- Healthcare technology

Brighton Park provides "growth equity reimagined" — combining capital with unique experience and founder empathy. They invest in only a handful of exceptional companies each year, providing undivided attention to portfolio companies.
