## Research Methodology

### Email Verification Sources (in order of preference):
1. **VERIFIED:** Official press releases, contact pages with published emails
2. **VERIFIED:** Multiple third-party verification services (ContactOut, RocketReach, ZoomInfo) showing full or near-full email
3. **Pattern Inferred:** Single third-party source showing partial email (e.g., j******@domain.com) + confirmed pattern from official team members
4. **Needs Verification:** Only general office contact found, no individual email

### Pattern Confirmation:
- When email pattern is inferred, cross-reference with:
  - Other confirmed contacts at the same firm
  - Industry standard patterns (first.last, firstlast, first+last)
  - Partial email reveals from verification services

### Notes on Generic Emails:
- ❌ Avoided: info@, sales@, ir@, contact@, businessdevelopment@
- ✓ Targeted: C-level, Partners, Managing Directors, VPs, Directors, Heads of departments
