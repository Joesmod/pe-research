## Firm History & Thesis
Founded in 1989 by **Louis Kelso**, the inventor of the Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). The firm is built on the principles of partnership and alignment of interest.

Kelso pioneered the use of ESOP structures in private equity, creating unique alignment between management, employees, and investors.

North American-focused middle market private equity firm with deep expertise in:
- Control buyouts
- Growth capital
- Recapitalizations
- ESOP transactions
