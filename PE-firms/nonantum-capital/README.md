## Investment Thesis

Nonantum partners with management teams to transform businesses. The firm brings decades of experience partnering with founders, families, and management teams to build businesses of enduring value.

**Name Origin:** "Nonantum" is the name of the village where the managing partner was raised. The name reflects a return to roots as investors — focusing on lower middle-market and family/founder businesses for which the team has a passion.
