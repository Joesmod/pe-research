## Company Overview
Alpine Investors is a people-driven private equity firm with the PeopleFirst philosophy at its core. They focus on attracting and retaining extraordinary people to drive results. The firm invests in software & technology services (predominantly DSO - "Destination Service Organizations").

## Investment Philosophy
**PeopleFirst:** Overarching investing and operating philosophy where the firm drives results by attracting and retaining extraordinary people. This philosophy shapes how they invest, develop talent, their recipe for growth, and their platform for impact.
