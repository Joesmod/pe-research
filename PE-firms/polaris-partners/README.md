# Polaris Partners

## Overview
Polaris Partners is a venture capital firm founded in 1996, active in healthcare, biotechnology, and technology companies. The firm operates two distinct investment strategies: Polaris Partners (early-stage healthcare/biotech VC) and Polaris Growth Fund (growth equity in technology/SaaS companies).

## Key Information
- **Founded**: 1996
- **Founders**: Jon Flint, Terry McGuire, Steve Arnold
- **Headquarters**: Boston, MA
- **Additional Offices**: New York, NY; San Francisco, CA
- **Website**: https://polarispartners.com
- **Focus**: Healthcare, Biotechnology (Polaris); Technology, SaaS (Polaris Growth Fund)

## Investment Strategies

### Polaris Partners (Main Fund)
- **Focus**: Early-stage healthcare and biotechnology
- **Stage**: Seed, Series A, Series B
- **Sectors**: Medical devices, diagnostics, health products, healthcare services
- **Approach**: Board members, entrepreneurs, scientists, operators
- **Track Record**: Co-founder of 20+ Polaris-backed companies

### Polaris Growth Fund
- **Lead**: Bryce Youngren (Managing Partner)
- **Focus**: Growth equity in technology companies
- **Sectors**: SaaS, B2B technology, tech-enabled services
- **Stage**: Growth equity, later-stage rounds
- **Approach**: Building category-leading technology companies
- **Recognition**: Named among best growth funds in the US
