## Value Creation Approach
Argosy employs a proprietary **Value Acceleration Methodology (VAM™)**, built from decades of growth-focused experience. The methodology focuses on:
1. **Growing Revenue** - Sales and market expansion
2. **Improving Margins** - Operational efficiency
3. **Professionalizing** - People, processes, and systems

The 8-person, full-time Operating Partner team provides portfolio company management teams with resources and tools to grow and professionalize their businesses.

## Differentiators
