## Background

Gauge Capital is a middle-market private equity firm that partners with owners, managers, and equity stakeholders of successful middle-market companies to maximize their potential.

## Strategy

Gauge's primary aim is long-term, sustainable value creation through:
1. Optimizing strategy and value creation agenda
2. Ensuring the right talent exists at management and board levels
3. Creating alignment among all stakeholders
4. Providing sufficient capital to achieve the company's vision

Focus on growth-oriented mid-market companies with high potential for organic and strategically acquired growth.
