## Overview

Founded in 2009 by John Shulman, Juggernaut Capital Partners is a private equity firm specializing in buyout and growth investments across the consumer, healthcare, and business services sectors. The firm focuses on lower middle market companies and "special situations" - unusual companies that may be more challenging to handle.

## Investment Focus

- **Consumer** (primary sector)
- **Healthcare** / Pharmaceutical
- **Business Services**
- **Special Situations**
