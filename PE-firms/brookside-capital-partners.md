## Firm Overview

Brookside Capital Partners (formerly Brookside Mezzanine Partners) is a private credit and mezzanine debt provider focused on the middle market. Founded in 2001 by David Buttolph and partners.

## Investment Focus

- **Sectors:** Business Services, Healthcare, Industrial
- **Stage:** Middle-market companies, typically in partnership with private equity sponsors
- **Capital Type:** Mezzanine debt, subordinated debt, junior capital
- **Investment Size:** $10M-$50M per transaction
- **Geography:** National

## Recent Activity

- **Fund V:** Successfully closed at $393 million (oversubscribed) in November 2024
- Total capital raised: $1.3B across all funds
- Name change from Brookside Mezzanine Partners to Brookside Capital Partners in 2021 to reflect expanded product offerings
