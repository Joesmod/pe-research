# GenNx360 Capital

## Overview
GenNx360 is a growth-oriented private equity firm with a fully-integrated team of operational and investment management experts. The firm drives value through Operational Engagement and Investment Excellence, focusing on middle-market companies in industrial and business services sectors.

- **Headquarters**: New York, NY (with offices in Boston and Austin)
