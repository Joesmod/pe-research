## Firm Overview

Trian Fund Management, L.P. (trading as Trian Partners) is a multi-billion dollar activist hedge fund founded in November 2005 by three principals: Nelson Peltz, Peter May, and Ed Garden. The firm is known for taking significant stakes in publicly traded companies and advocating for operational improvements and strategic changes.

## Investment Strategy

- **Type:** Activist investing
- **Focus:** Large-cap public companies with untapped potential
- **Approach:** Highly engaged shareholder with board representation
- **Philosophy:** Entrepreneurial spirit, deep operational expertise, ownership mentality
- **Stage:** Public and private investments
