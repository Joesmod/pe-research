## Enrichment Notes

### Email Patterns Observed
- Most PE firms follow: `firstinitial+lastname@firm.com` or `firstname@firm.com` for founders
- Some use full firstname: `christopher@burchcreativecapital.com`
- Shorter firms often use initials: `ade@base10.vc`, `gjain@afore.vc`

### Best Sources for Verified Contacts
1. **Emerging Manager Monthly (EMM)** - Directory with direct emails for minority/diverse managers
2. **ContactOut** - High accuracy for direct emails
3. **RocketReach** - Good for pattern confirmation
4. **Official team pages** - Bio pages often reveal email patterns
5. **Apollo.io** - Good for mid-level contacts

### Red Flags (Avoided)
- Generic inquiry emails (info@, ir@, sales@) - not decision-makers
- Patterns without verification sources
- Contact aggregator sites with no source attribution
