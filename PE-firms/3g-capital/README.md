## Overview

3G Capital is a global investment firm focused on long-term value creation through operational improvements and strategic growth. Founded by Brazilian billionaires Jorge Paulo Lemann, Carlos Alberto Sicupira, and Marcel Herrmann Telles, 3G Capital is known for its aggressive cost-cutting and zero-based budgeting approach.

**Notable Investments:**  
- Burger King (acquired 2010, merged with Tim Hortons to form Restaurant Brands International)  
- Heinz (acquired 2013 with Berkshire Hathaway)  
- Kraft Foods (merged with Heinz to form Kraft Heinz in 2015)  
- Anheuser-Busch InBev (involvement through partners)

**Investment Strategy:**  
- Operational excellence and cost efficiency  
- Zero-based budgeting methodology  
- Long-term value creation  
- Focus on consumer brands and food & beverage
