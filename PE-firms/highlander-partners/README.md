## Overview
Highlander Partners is a Dallas-based private investment firm with over $3.0 billion in assets under management. The firm is uniquely positioned by using its own proprietary capital for investments (no limited partners). Founded in April 2004 by Laurence E. Hirsch and team, bringing over 40 years of operational and investment experience.

## Unique Structure
- Manages own proprietary capital
- No limited partners whose varying interests can weigh on the investment process
- Global investment platform with operational focus
- Integrated team of operational and investment professionals
