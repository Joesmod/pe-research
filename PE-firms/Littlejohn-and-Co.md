## Investment Strategy
Littlejohn seeks to identify, invest in, and support great companies on their next phase of transformational growth. The firm leverages deep operational expertise and focuses on companies undergoing change in capital structure, strategy, operations, or growth that can benefit from its operational and strategic approach.
