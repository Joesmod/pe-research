## Overview

AUA Private Equity Partners is a consumer-focused private equity firm specializing in investments in Hispanic-oriented companies and family-owned businesses seeking liquidity or transition. Founded in 2011 by Managing Partner Andy Unanue (former Goya Foods executive), the firm brings operational expertise and cultural insights to transforming family businesses into leading institutional companies.
