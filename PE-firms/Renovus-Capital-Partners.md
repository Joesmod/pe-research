## Investment Thesis
Renovus Capital Partners is a mid-market private equity firm focused on "knowledge and talent" driven businesses. The firm targets investments in companies with $3-15 million EBITDA across four core verticals:

1. **Education** - Training and educational services
2. **Healthcare Services** - Provider services, tech-enabled healthcare
3. **Professional Services** - Business services, consulting
4. **Human Capital** - Workforce development, recruiting, staffing

The firm typically partners with founder-led businesses and leverages operational expertise to drive growth through:
- Operational improvements
- Top talent recruitment
- Add-on acquisitions
- Strategic growth initiatives
