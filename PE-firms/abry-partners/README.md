## Investment Strategy
Abry Partners is a middle-market private equity firm specializing in buyouts, growth equity, and credit investments. The firm focuses on companies in:
- **Media & Communications**: Cable, satellite, content, digital media
- **Business Services**: Information services, BPO, tech-enabled services
- **Healthcare Services**: Healthcare IT, services, and related businesses
- **Infrastructure**: Telecom infrastructure, towers, fiber

Recent strategic initiatives include launching a CLO (collateralized loan obligation) platform, expanding credit capabilities.
