## Background
Jeffrey B. Schwartz founded Corbel Capital Partners in 2013 and currently serves as the Firm's Managing Partner and Chief Executive Officer. Corbel is known for providing creative structured capital to middle-market businesses.

The leadership team has collectively led and overseen over 100 transactions totaling over $8 billion, with significant experience partnering with lower middle market companies. With the assistance of its operating advisors, Corbel seeks to add value to portfolio companies through operational improvement, strategic, M&A and capital structure direction, and relationships.
