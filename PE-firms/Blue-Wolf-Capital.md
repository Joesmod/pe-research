## Background

Blue Wolf Capital Partners was founded in 2005 by Adam Blumenthal and Josh Wolf-Powers. The firm specializes in middle-market private equity, driving operational excellence through innovative solutions in industrial and healthcare sectors.

Over three decades of experience, with principals serving on boards of numerous middle-market companies.

## Strategy

Blue Wolf targets high-quality middle-market companies within niche industries across:
- Industrial sector (healthcare, forest & building products, manufacturing, industrial services, engineering services)
- Healthcare sector

Focus on aligning companies with public market comparables to unlock long-term growth.

Transforming complexity into value through operational excellence and innovative solutions.
