## Overview

The Riverside Company is a global private equity firm focused on the smaller end of the middle market. Founded in 1988, the firm has invested in over 960 companies globally. With approximately 350 employees and more than $14 billion in assets under management, Riverside is one of the largest and most experienced firms dedicated to this market segment.
