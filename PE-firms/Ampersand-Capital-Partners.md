## Investment Focus
Ampersand is a middle-market private equity firm dedicated to growth-oriented investments in the healthcare and life sciences sectors. The firm invests in companies that support the life sciences ecosystem, including:
- Laboratory Products
- Laboratory Services
- Contract Manufacturing
- Pharma Services
- Specialty Products
