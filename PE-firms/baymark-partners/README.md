# Baymark Partners

**Website:** https://baymarkpartners.com  
**Location:** Dallas, Texas  
**Founded:** ~2010s  
**Focus:** Middle-market PE investing in services, healthcare, IT, distribution, and tech-enabled businesses

## Overview

Baymark Partners is a Dallas-based growth-oriented private equity firm that acquires growing middle-market companies in the services sector. The firm provides owners with liquidity and companies with resources to accelerate their growth.

## Investment Criteria

- **Target Sectors:**
  - Healthcare services
  - Business services
  - IT services
  - Distribution
  - Light manufacturing
  - Tech-enabled businesses (SaaS, e-commerce)

- **Deal Size:** Middle-market (typically $1M-$50M enterprise value)
- **AUM:** ~$165M
- **Investment Type:** Majority and minority recapitalizations, management buyouts
