## Overview
Bow River Capital is a specialized investment firm focused on the Mountain West "Rodeo Region" with expertise across private equity, software growth equity, asset-based finance, and real estate. The firm emphasizes partnerships with business owners and management teams that share their values.

## Strategies
1. **Private Equity** - Lower middle market focus
2. **Software Growth Equity** - SaaS and technology companies
3. **Asset-Based Finance** - Lending and structured finance
4. **Real Estate** - Property investments and development
5. **ONE Bow River** - Evergreen platform
6. **Private Credit** - Debt investments

## Investment Criteria
- **Geography:** "Rodeo Region" (Mountain West U.S.)
- **Philosophy:** Partnership-focused, values alignment, operational support
- **Support:** Dedicated finance, compliance, and operations teams
- **Approach:** "Innovation, nimbleness and partnership of a boutique investment firm with the capability and commitment of institutional-quality operational support"
