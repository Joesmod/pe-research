# QHP Capital

## Overview
QHP Capital (formerly NovaQuest Private Equity) is a healthcare-focused private equity firm partnering with management teams to build leading healthcare companies in the middle market. The firm focuses on transforming market leaders into larger, better positioned, and more valuable enterprises.

## Key Information
- **Founded**: Formerly NovaQuest Private Equity; rebranded as QHP Capital in 2022
- **Headquarters**: Research Triangle Park, North Carolina
- **Website**: https://www.qhpcapital.com
- **Focus**: Life sciences, pharma services, healthcare middle market
- **Heritage**: Connected to Quintiles (now IQVIA) and NovaQuest

## Investment Strategy
- **Sector Focus**: Life sciences, pharma services companies
- **Stage**: Middle market, proven products/services
- **Approach**: Strategic platform for sourcing, diligence, value creation
- **Value Creation**: Operational improvements, commercial scalability, strategic initiatives
