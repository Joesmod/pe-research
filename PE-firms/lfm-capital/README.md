## Overview
LFM Capital is a Nashville-based private equity firm founded by operators and engineers. The firm invests in manufacturing companies in the U.S. and Canada, bringing deep operational expertise to support portfolio companies. They are on a mission to establish long-term partnerships with management teams and investors.
