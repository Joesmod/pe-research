# Grafine Partners

**Website:** https://grafine.com/  
**Founded:** 2019  
**Headquarters:** New York, NY  
**Type:** Boutique Alternative Asset Management Firm

## Overview

Grafine Partners is a specialized investment firm focused on sourcing off-market, direct private equity deal flow for sophisticated capital sources across the globe. The firm takes a creative approach to investing private capital and partners with the next generation of private equity leaders to source distinctive private investment opportunities.

## Investment Focus

- **Financial Services**
- **GP Stakes** (General Partner equity investments)
- **Direct Investments** for institutional investors

## Business Model

Grafine operates as an institutional direct investment platform, serving clients such as:
- Sovereign Wealth Funds (SWFs)
- Large family offices
- Endowments

The firm was created to meet the needs of investors who prefer direct investments over traditional blind pool PE funds with 2 & 20 fee structures.
