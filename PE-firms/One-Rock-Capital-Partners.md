## Background
One Rock Capital Partners is an operationally-focused private equity firm. The firm utilizes the expertise of its Operating Partners and global resources of its strategic partner, Mitsubishi Corporation, to execute upon value-creation initiatives alongside management teams.

Tony Lee was previously a Managing Director at Ripplewood Holdings, where he joined in 1997 and was responsible for Ripplewood's efforts in the global chemicals and industrial sectors. Prior to Ripplewood, he worked in the Mergers and Acquisitions Group at Salomon Brothers.

Scott Spielvogel is a co-founder and Managing Partner known for his operational expertise and value creation focus.
