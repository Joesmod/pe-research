## Investment Strategy
Lightyear Capital is a sector-specialist private equity firm focused almost exclusively on financial services, insurance, fintech infrastructure, and wealth management platforms. The firm targets middle-market companies within its specialized verticals, bringing deep industry expertise and operational resources.

**Target Sectors:**
- **Financial Services**: Banking, lending, payments
- **Insurance**: Property & casualty, specialty insurance, MGAs
- **Fintech**: Financial technology infrastructure, payments platforms
- **Wealth Management**: RIAs, wealth platforms, asset management technology

The firm leverages its extensive network of financial services executives and advisors to add operational value to portfolio companies.
