## Investment Strategy
Incline Equity Partners is a lower middle-market private equity firm that partners with established companies that have opportunities for growth. They focus on three core sectors:
- Value-added Distribution
- Business and Industrial Services
- Specialized Light Manufacturing

The firm takes a high-intensity, fun-loving approach to value creation and works fast in pursuit of operational improvements and growth.
