# PE Research Repository

## Overview
This repository contains enriched profiles and dossiers for private equity firms, focusing on mid-market PE firms with service-heavy portfolio strategies.

**Mission:** Generate qualified leads with verified contacts for Hello Gumbo PE outreach.

## Firm Dossier Format

Each dossier includes:
- **Firm Overview:** Name, type, focus, AUM, location
- **Investment Strategy:** Sectors, deal size, geography
- **Team:** Leadership, key decision-makers, titles
- **Contact Information:** Domain, phone, available emails
- **Outreach Strategy:** Recommended approach based on availability
- **Research Status:** What's verified, what's missing
- **Last Updated:** Date and researcher

## Research Standards

### Email Verification
- ✅ **Use:** Emails from official press releases, SEC filings, official websites
- ✅ **Use:** Generic firm emails (info@, bd@, contact@) from official sites
- ❌ **Never:** Guess email patterns
- ❌ **Never:** Use unverified third-party sources without notation
- ❌ **Never:** Hallucinate or infer contact details

### Source Priority
1. Official firm website
2. Official press releases (PR Newswire, Business Wire, etc.)
3. SEC filings (for public companies)
4. LinkedIn (if email shown on profile)
5. Industry directories (ACG, PitchBook) - note source
6. Third-party aggregators - **flag as unverified**

### Status Flags
- ✅ Verified from official source
- ⚠️ Found but unverified (note source)
- ❌ Not publicly available
- 🔍 Needs further research

## Key Findings

### Email Availability Challenge
95%+ of mid-market PE firms **do not publish** individual email addresses on their websites.

**Common contact methods instead:**
- Website contact forms
- Generic emails (info@, contact@, ir@)
- Main office phone numbers
- LinkedIn direct messages

**Implication:** Traditional email-based outreach requires:
1. Phone calls to main office
2. LinkedIn outreach
3. Industry event connections
4. Warm introductions through network

### Successful Approaches
1. **Business Development Contacts:** Some firms list BD/deal origination team members - target these
2. **Recent Press Releases:** New deals/launches sometimes include media contacts
3. **New Firms:** Recently launched firms may be more accessible
4. **Portfolio Company Connections:** Reach portfolio company leadership first
5. **Industry Events:** ACG, middle-market conferences = face-to-face access

## Target Firm Profile

**Ideal characteristics for Hello Gumbo outreach:**
- Mid-market PE ($500M-$5B AUM)
- Services-heavy portfolio
- Value creation/portfolio ops focus
- Seeking operational improvements
- Multiple platform companies
- Active deal pipeline

**Decision-makers to target:**
- C-level: CEO, COO, CTO, CMO
- Partners: Managing, Operating, General
- Directors: Technology, Product, Operations, Digital
- VPs: Portfolio Ops, Value Creation, Digital Transformation
- Heads of: Business Development, Portfolio Operations

## Contributing

When adding new dossiers:
1. Use the existing template format
2. Verify all facts from official sources
3. Flag unverified information with ⚠️
4. Note research date and your name
5. Include source URLs where available
6. Mark research status (✅/⚠️/❌/🔍)
