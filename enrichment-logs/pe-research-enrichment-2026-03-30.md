## Research Methodology

**Sources Used:**
- Official firm websites (team pages, contact pages)
- Official press releases (BusinessWire, PRNewswire, firm news sections)
- Apollo.io API (verified status contacts)
- ContactOut (published contact data)
- RocketReach/ZoomInfo (email pattern verification, NOT as primary source)
- LinkedIn (role confirmation)

**Email Verification Levels:**
- ✅ VERIFIED = Found on official website, press release, or published source
- Pattern Inferred = Based on verified company email patterns (firstname.lastname@, first@, etc.)

**Quality Standards:**
- ONLY used emails from official published sources or high-confidence patterns
- NEVER guessed email patterns without verification
- Left blank when no reliable source available
- Noted source for each contact in enrichment notes
